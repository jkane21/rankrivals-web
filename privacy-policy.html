RankRivals Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

RankRivals is a fantasy football rankings and scoring app operated by Jackson Kane ("RankRivals," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at rankrivalsapp@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account and identity. RankRivals requires you to sign in with Apple. Sign in with Apple asks your permission to share your name and email address with the app. We store:

  • An identifier for your Apple account, which we use to recognize you on future sign-ins.
  • Your email address, if you choose to share it. Apple may provide a private relay address instead of your real address; either works.

We do not retain your name from Sign in with Apple.

Profile. During setup you provide a display name and handle, and choose an avatar color. You are also asked an optional "how did you hear about us?" question and whether you want to receive product email. We store your answers to these.

Your activity in the app. We store the content you create in RankRivals: your weekly player rankings, the pools you create or join, and your alert preferences.

Notifications. If you enable push notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can deliver the alerts you turned on (such as a rankings-lock reminder or pool activity). You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings or within the app.

Subscriptions. Paid subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and managed with RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. We receive your subscription status (which plan you have and whether it is active) so the app can unlock the right features. Apple handles all payment processing — we never receive your payment card details.

What we do not collect. RankRivals does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history, and does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking software. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: score your rankings, build leaderboards, and run pools you participate in.
  • To deliver the notifications you have enabled.
  • To process and restore your subscription.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To send product email, only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • To protect the service against fraud, abuse, and security threats, and to comply with legal obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers that operate the app on our behalf: Apple (Sign in with Apple and subscription billing), RevenueCat (subscription management), Expo (push-notification delivery), Supabase (database hosting), and Render (server hosting). These providers may process your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Other users, in a limited way: people in a pool with you can see your display name, handle, avatar, and your standings in that pool.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, or to investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, or harm.
  • Business transfer: if RankRivals is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Third-party sports data

RankRivals retrieves the NFL schedule, game scores and player stats, betting lines, and stadium weather from third-party data providers (currently ESPN, The Odds API, and OpenWeatherMap). These requests are made by our servers and do not include any of your personal information.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, rankings, pool memberships, alert preferences, and stored push token. We may retain a minimal record where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, or deletion. Email rankrivalsapp@gmail.com to request a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account.
  • Notifications. Manage them in iOS Settings or in the app.
  • Product email. Opt out using the link in any such email, or by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what we hold about you and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any right, contact us at the address above; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

Children

RankRivals is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International users

RankRivals is operated from the United States, and your information is processed and stored there. If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above. If the changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice (for example, in the app).

Contact

Questions or requests: rankrivalsapp@gmail.com
